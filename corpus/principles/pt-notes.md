# Physical therapy home programs: notes

Source: `physical_therapy_routines/2018 PT`, `2020 PT`, `2026 PT`. Only the 2026 file names its target ("Lumbar PT Exercises"); the 2018 and 2020 files are titled "Home Program – Workout Routine" and state no diagnosis.

## What each program contained

- **2018 (13 exercises).** Trunk stability: bird dog with band, dead bug with swiss ball, standard plank (5x weekly, "add punches, rotations of the feet"), side plank on elbow, wall multifidus. Hip stabilizers: monster walk and a clamshell series (clamshell, reverse clamshell, clamshell in abduction). Lumbar extension: prone press up, 2x daily. Mobility: foam roller thoracic series, doorway hamstring stretch, hip flexor stretch at edge of bed.
- **2020 (13 exercises, episode of care from 11/20/2020).** Opens with prone press up ("add exhale on press") and a right-side standing lateral shift correction at the wall. Stretches for piriformis (figure 4), quadriceps, and thoracic rotation (open book). Strength and control 3x weekly: figure 4 bridge, plank ("add foam roll"), Forward T single-leg balance, side plank on full arm with rotation, runner's step up/down. Ends with plyometrics: lateral hopping and jump lunges. Monster walk listed at 3x daily.
- **2026 (4 exercises, all 1x daily, 2 x 15).** Quadruped train tracks, TA march, split stance single arm row with thoracic rotation, supine hip internal/external rotation.

## Recurring themes

- Trunk held still while a limb moves: bird dog, dead bug, TA march, train tracks, the row ("maintain stillness in your legs and pelvis").
- Low back position: repeated "keep your low back flat" and "do not let your low back arch" (dead bug, hip flexor stretch, quad stretch, TA march, hip rotation).
- Hips stay square: "do not roll your hips forward or backward" (clamshells), "do not let your hips rotate" (bridge, side plank), "hips should stay facing forward" (open book).
- Knee alignment in standing and landing work: "don't let your knee collapse inward" (Forward T, step up/down, hops, jump lunges).
- Prone press up and plank appear in both 2018 and 2020.

## Stated cautions

- Foam roller series: if snow angels bother your shoulder(s), lift arms off ground more or remove that motion.
- Hamstring stretch: do not lock your knee; keep breathing evenly.
- Jump lunges: do not let knees move forward past your toes on landing.

## 2. Why Top 2: The Movement System + PJF Performance

Pairing **The Movement System** (`Dr. Matt Casturo, DPT, CSCS`) with **PJF Performance** (`Paul Fabritz, CSCS`) covers both halves of the corpus requirement with zero redundancy:

1. **Quantitative Exercise Physiology & PT Guardrails (The Movement System)**:
   - Supplies explicit NSCA CSCS 5th-edition ground-contact volume tables per session and per week (`zLXua2DzQ1o`).
   - Defines fast (`<250ms`) vs slow (`>250ms`) stretch-shortening cycle mechanics, amortization efficiency, and work-to-rest ratios (`xeR4RprByc4`).
   - Gives a 5-stage plyometric progression gate from stick landings to depth jumps (`_YudFDcW_Iw`), force-plate RFD/RSI diagnostic metrics (`11uS8bwkfcc`), 3-phase strength-to-plyo periodization (`1CFAZfxEkoQ`), and the `≥70% MVIC` tendon strain threshold (`h8zKS5qZZdY`).
2. **Basketball Jump Biomechanics & Late-30s/40s Tendon Load Management (PJF Performance)**:
   - Distinguishes standing vertical jump mechanics from 2-foot and 1-foot basketball approach jumps (`wrF0h4YadvM`) and provides a simple field test (standing vs approach differential) to set the athlete's strength-vs-plyo training ratio (`k4QD_g3nkaU`).
   - Directly solves the recreational adult hooper's main failure mode—stacking plyometrics on top of pickup basketball and heavy lifting until patellar/Achilles tendinopathy flares—via the **Tendon Jerk Budget** (`mNbpZ9-7XM0`), net collagen synthesis recovery curves (`NF658w-s8io`), isometric + HSR jumper's knee protocols (`6oBTSuxggLc`), and low-impact concentric power substitutions for low-readiness/cranky-knee days (`AR1JI0RFsbQ`).
